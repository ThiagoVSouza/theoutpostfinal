Tree ground and trunk contact pass
=================================

Purpose
-------
At the closest gameplay view (40 m), the previous soil texture's fine debris
averaged into a pale, nearly continuous island. The trunk ended abruptly against
that island. This pass gives the ground larger readable shapes and connects the
existing trunk to them, without adding props or changing the tree geometry.

Artwork
-------
assets/textures/tree_ground_variants.png is a transparent 2 x 2 atlas made with
the built-in ImageGen tool. See tree_ground_variants_prompt.txt for the exact
prompts, reference roles, and source image record. The previous
tree_ground_patch.png and its prompt are retained as the earlier version.

The four cells vary the balance of exposed soil, low green cover, stone marks,
and litter. Top-left is airy broadleaf ground, top-right is earthier broadleaf
ground, bottom-left is dry conifer litter, and bottom-right is mossier ground.
Transparent holes let the actual meadow show through. These are flat painted
suggestions of vegetation, stones, and roots; there are no additional objects.

Runtime
-------
The ground remains one two-triangle surface per tree, using the existing species
and chunk MultiMeshes. Variation is stable for each tree and uses its transform;
it does not animate with the camera or require per-frame CPU updates.

A narrow contact treatment follows each trunk's footprint at ground level.
The lowest part of the existing bark receives a short dirt/moss transition.
Above that transition the bark keeps its original texture and material values.
The GLBs, their vertex positions, and their triangle counts remain unchanged.

Ground edges sample the same grass and material settings as the Board. The base
supports both the original grass contrast path and the newer separate cloud/blade
contrast path. Board shader overrides and defaults are copied at initialization;
the new path is selected only when the Board exposes its corresponding uniforms.
The Board shader and scene lighting are not changed by this pass.

The grass/hold functions remain local to the base shader because the project's
Godot 4.7.1 loader-thread issue prevents safely sharing them through an include.
When the Board grass algorithm changes, update the corresponding base path too.

Visual validation
-----------------
Use the real Vulkan renderer for screenshots, not the headless renderer.
Start with birch and oak at the 40 m gameplay view, then inspect the species
gallery and a 10,000-tree forest. Check the trunk foot, broken paint outline,
green/stone readability, alpha padding, and consistency with the surrounding
grass. Compare identical views before and after; retain the captures under
the ignored previews/tree-visual-pass directory.

The production smoke check covers 1,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 / off, Detail/Distant
mesh and canopy-map transitions, and exact per-species triangle counts:
    Oak 199, Maple 199, Birch 199, Pine 200, Stone pine 199,
    Spruce 197, Cypress 197, White willow 199.

Also check the newer meadow grade and lighting independently, without including
unrelated scene or terrain edits in this change.
