TREE VISUAL PASS
===============

The eight original trees remain the source assets. tools/art/refine_trees.py reads
their GLBs from the pinned original revision (2f193f8), then changes card placement,
volume normals, vertex occlusion and bark/leaf tints. Trunk positions and normals
remain exactly as authored; all foliage cards stay in the crown.
It preserves index buffers and triangle counts. Embedded images and UVs also stay
unchanged except for oak, maple, birch, stone pine and white willow foliage. Pine
replaces only its foliage image with the original v1 texture, preserving its UVs.
The five branch-spray atlases are assets/trees/textures/oak-boughs.png,
maple-boughs.png, birch-boughs.png, stone-pine-boughs.png and white-willow-boughs.png;
each uses four matching UV regions.
Run from the repository root with Python 3 (standard library only):

    python tools/art/refine_trees.py

This is deterministic and can be rerun after editing the species parameters.
The generated GLBs are committed assets; Python is not needed to run the game.
To regenerate just one species:

    python tools/art/refine_trees.py --species stone-pine

Oak: 66 existing foliage quads use four broad branching sprays with rounded-lobed
leaves. The earlier single branch repeated very fine leaf detail across the crown,
reading as stippled clumps at gameplay distance. Larger connected leaf groups and
distinct transparent branch channels improve that readability. Oak shares birch's
source card orientation: keep V on primary plates and flip V on both side plates.
The side sprays retain their vertical positions, so their outward tips descend
naturally around the broad spreading crown. An inward radial stretch connects
middle-crown boughs: each group's outer radial edge stays fixed, while the interior
extends inward by up to 40 percent of its radial span. This fades out toward outer
and upper groups, preserving overall bounds, height and the peripheral silhouette.
Wood, indices and the ground patch remain unchanged. Normals use the resulting
positions and blend 40 percent local shape with 60 percent crown
envelope to reduce separately rounded clump shading. Runtime texture settings use
high-quality VRAM compression, mipmaps and alpha-border repair.
See oak_boughs_prompt.txt for the exact generation and layout-refinement prompts.

Stone pine: the earlier whole-clump atlas repeated three dense, rounded pads at
each bough. The replacement has asymmetric needle-bearing sprays and transparent
channels, so branch structure survives the closest gameplay view. Its 75 foliage
quads use complementary atlas regions; the wood is unchanged. Stone-pine normals
weight the shared crown envelope 75 percent, reducing the inflated look of each
separate pad. Sprays face outward, and their larger central overlap connects the
core while leaving a broken fringe. Godot's extracted runtime atlas uses
high-quality VRAM compression and mipmaps.
See stone_pine_boughs_prompt.txt for the exact texture-generation prompt.

Maple: 66 existing foliage quads carry complementary branches with pointed,
five-lobed leaves and visible twigs. Its original rounded canopy depth is retained;
slightly larger sprays, especially inside its three-dimensional crown envelope,
keep the broadleaf crown full while allowing gaps between branches. Normals use
40 percent local bough shape and 60 percent crown envelope. The original wood,
ground patch, index buffers and triangle counts are preserved. Runtime atlas import
settings match the stone pine. See maple_boughs_prompt.txt for the exact prompt.
White willow: 66 existing quads carry fine branching sprays with narrow pointed
leaves and muted silvery undersides. The primary card in each bough points its
painted tip outward/down through its V flip. The other two cards reflect vertically
around their own centers so their tips also descend; their centers, dimensions and
the broad crown remain intact. Slightly larger interior sprays connect the crown,
while normals use 35 percent local shape and 65 percent crown envelope. Original
wood, indices and the ground patch remain unchanged. Runtime texture settings match
maple and stone pine. See white_willow_boughs_prompt.txt for the exact prompt.

Birch: 66 existing quads carry small pointed, serrated leaves on fine branching
twigs. Its source cards face the opposite way to the other atlas species: primary
cards keep V, while the two side cards flip V to point painted tips outward. Cards
whose outward direction descends reflect vertically around their own centers, so
all sprays rise gently away from the slender crown. Original card scale and crown
lift remain, with at most eight percent extra interior overlap and no outer growth.
Normals use 40 percent local shape and 60 percent crown envelope. White-bark wood,
indices and the ground patch remain unchanged; runtime texture settings match the
other branch atlases. A four-percent UV inset inside each atlas cell uses its extra
transparent padding, retaining safe margins while keeping the leafy body full.
See birch_boughs_prompt.txt for the three exact generation/refinement prompts.
Pine: restore the v1 needle texture and keep all 58 complete boughs (26 folded pairs
and 32 planar quads). Transforming each half-fold independently had separated their
shared joins. The dedicated pine recipe groups connected strips first, pins all
252 root-edge vertices, and keeps all fold joins exact. Lower and middle boughs
grow up to six percent in length and fifteen percent in outer width, fading to no
growth at the upper leader. Crown height, UVs, bark image and wood are unchanged.
Normals blend 60 percent upward source-card shading, 25 percent crown envelope and
15 percent complete-bough volume, preserving readable needle-bearing surfaces.
This restores connected fullness toward the exact oaklab-pine v1 comparison while
retaining the current 30-triangle textured trunk and 200-triangle total budget.
The v1 texture has finer needle fringes than the dense v2 texture; an A/B render
with identical geometry and shading confirmed softer tips without thinning the
crown. assets/trees/textures/pine-bough-v1.png is the unmodified embedded image 0
from oaklab-pine/oaklab-pine.glb in TheOutpostModels/local-models/neutral/nature.
The original 1536 x 1024 PNG is copied directly, with no pixel edits.

Forest appends the base as a shared third material surface in each species mesh,
using the existing chunk MultiMesh transforms. It adds one surface draw per visible
species/chunk, not one node or draw per tree. The ImageGen-created
tree_ground_patch.png paints exposed earth, dust, leaf litter and small pebbles
around the unchanged trunk (see tree_ground_patch_prompt.txt for the exact prompt).
All ground detail is flat artwork. Its alpha blends the paint into the exact Board
grass colour before lighting, while alpha scissor discards the transparent border.
It stays in the opaque rendering path and receives the scene's tree shadows.
The patch rotates with each tree, with stable UV flips, slight scale differences
and subtle brightness variation. Texture and shader are loaded once in _ready().
The base shader keeps three texture reads: two for matching grass and one for paint.
The paint uses high-quality VRAM compression, mipmaps and alpha-border repair.

Foliage COLOR_0 supplies direction-independent bough shading; all eight assets now
carry outpostFoliageNormalMode="volume". The shader preserves the volume normals on
both card faces. Distant canopy color also includes area-weighted vertex color.

Grass uses the ImageGen-created grass_meadow_detail.png (see grass_meadow_prompt.txt
for both exact prompts and source roles). The original grass_meadow.png remains the
macro texture. The two texture reads now use independent 30 x 27.5 m and
657 x 602.25 m periods. Detail RGB is corrected by measured linear-light mean ratios
so the texture change does not shift the whole board's average palette. Import uses
VRAM compression, high quality and mipmaps.

Keep the grass()/hold() implementations and matching default uniforms in
shaders/board.gdshader and shaders/tree_base.gdshader synchronized. The intentional
duplication avoids the project's documented loader-thread shader-include issue.
Forest copies explicit Board material overrides; Godot returns null for unoverridden
shader defaults, so those defaults must match in both shaders.

Lighting: ambient .65, rim .16, sun 1.08; existing directions and ACES exposure retained.
The ground compensation new_light is measured, not estimated:
    (1.69575, 1.67591, 1.34783)
The review tool measures 16 linear-albedo strips with linear tonemapping and fits
their sRGB-decoded readback. Recalibrate after changing scene lighting.

Each capture writes a JSON with renderer, primitive/draw counts and measured CPU/GPU
times. Dense mode deliberately keeps all meshes visible beyond the normal detail
LOD, using Forest.set_count and its production chunking. It is a controlled stress
comparison, not a gameplay frame-rate claim. Baseline and final PNGs are in the
ignored previews/tree-visual-pass directory of the working checkout.
